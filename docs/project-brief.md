# Forsa — Project Brief

## Purpose
Build a premium, serious and trustworthy digital presence for Forsa.

## Positioning
Forsa works with executive coaching, leadership development and management team coaching for companies in Gothenburg and the Swedish market.

## Audience
- CEOs
- Management teams
- Founders
- Senior leaders
- HR and organisational decision-makers

## Core associations
- Clarity
- Direction
- Depth
- Trust
- Leadership maturity
- Better decisions
- Strategic movement

## Tone
Professional, calm, sharp, Nordic, human and serious.

## Avoid
- Generic coaching language
- Overly spiritual language
- Startup-style hype
- Too much “personal development” feeling
- Artificial corporate jargon

## Initial tagline direction
Clarity for leaders. Direction for companies.

## Swedish positioning draft
Forsa hjälper företagsledningar att tänka klarare, fatta bättre beslut och skapa riktning i förändring.
