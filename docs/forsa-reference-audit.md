# Forsa — reference audit from supplied sites

## Purpose
These references should be used to understand the coaching/leadership market around Gothenburg and Sweden. They are inspiration and positioning references only. Do not copy text directly.

## Reference 1: Sundborn Coaching

### What it signals
- Personal, warm and approachable coaching.
- Strong emphasis on individual coaching, personal development, self-awareness and change.
- Credibility through certification, civil engineering background and experience from Volvo/HCL.
- Local relevance: Göteborg, Sisjön, Kullavik, online.

### Useful learnings for Forsa
- Local Göteborg anchoring matters.
- Certification and background can build trust.
- The idea of an independent external conversation partner is relevant for leaders.
- Coaching for leaders can be framed as clarity, self-leadership and better decisions.

### What Forsa should avoid
- Too much private-person coaching language.
- Too soft phrases such as “hitta dina egna svar”, “personlig utveckling”, “full potential” as primary framing.
- Too much focus on the coach as a person rather than the executive/business outcome.

## Reference 2: Trust Your Talent

### What it signals
- Strongest commercial/organisational positioning among the references.
- Uses business language around talent, trust, performance, responsibility, growth and change.
- Connects leadership and human potential to measurable organisational energy and results.
- More modern than the others in tone.

### Useful learnings for Forsa
- Strong abstract idea works when tied to business: “trust as business force”.
- Leadership copy can talk about organisation, not only the individual leader.
- Good direction: “right person, right place, right playing field”.
- Relevant themes: trust, responsibility, contribution, change, performance.

### What Forsa should avoid
- Avoid becoming too slogan-heavy.
- Avoid broad talent-development language if the primary buyer is executive leadership.
- Avoid sounding like HR/talent consulting rather than executive coaching/advisory.

## Reference 3: Anna Vilkas

### What it signals
- Strongest seniority and authority.
- Uses credentials heavily: MCC, HR director, change management, international background, author, speaker, references.
- Clear leadership and business-development relevance.
- Very high energy and personality-led tone.

### Useful learnings for Forsa
- Senior credibility is essential when targeting CEOs and leadership teams.
- Referencing experience, methods, confidentiality and business understanding matters.
- Strong testimonials/recommendations are valuable.
- The content should show that the coach understands organisations, not only people.

### What Forsa should avoid
- Too much personality-driven copy.
- Too many credentials in the hero section.
- Long, dense, expressive paragraphs.
- Words like “glädje”, “lust”, “attraktiv flock” if Forsa wants a calmer executive tone.

# Strategic conclusion for Forsa

Forsa should position itself as a serious, calm and executive-level coaching/advisory company for leadership teams and senior decision-makers.

Forsa should not feel like general personal coaching. It should feel like an external thinking partner for leaders who need clarity, better decisions and direction in complex situations.

## Recommended positioning direction

Forsa helps leadership teams think clearly, make better decisions and create direction when the stakes are high.

## Swedish core line

Forsa hjälper företagsledningar att tänka klarare, fatta bättre beslut och skapa riktning när mycket står på spel.

## Alternative Swedish lines

- Executive coaching för ledare som behöver klarhet, riktning och bättre beslut.
- Ett externt samtalsrum för ledare och ledningsgrupper där riktning, ansvar och beslut blir tydligare.
- För företagsledningar som vill gå från otydlighet till riktning, från reaktion till medvetna beslut.
- När ledningen behöver tänka klarare, prata ärligare och agera mer samlat.

## Recommended Forsa tone

- Calm
- Sharp
- Senior
- Nordic
- Trustworthy
- Human but not soft
- Business-aware
- Reflective but not spiritual
- Premium without being inflated

## Words to use

# Suggested site structure

## Home
Purpose: Establish Forsa as a premium executive coaching and leadership advisory partner.

Sections:
1. Hero: clarity, decisions, direction.
2. Problem: leadership is often not lacking competence, but shared clarity.
3. What Forsa does: executive coaching, leadership team coaching, advisory conversations.
4. For whom: CEOs, founders, executive teams, senior leaders.
5. How it works: structured confidential conversations, clear direction, practical next steps.
6. Credibility: experience, confidentiality, business understanding, methods.
7. Contact CTA.

## Executive coaching
Purpose: Individual coaching for CEOs, founders and senior leaders.

Themes:
- Decision pressure
- Role clarity
- Self-leadership
- Change
- Difficult conversations
- Prioritisation
- Leadership maturity

## Leadership team coaching
Purpose: Work with management teams.

Themes:
- Shared direction
- Trust and responsibility
- Decision quality
- Friction and alignment
- Role clarity
- Strategic focus

## About
Purpose: Build trust around the person/people behind Forsa without making the site too personal.

Include:
- Background
- Experience
- Why Forsa exists
- Approach
- Confidentiality
- Who Forsa is best suited for

## Contact
Purpose: Simple, discreet conversion.

CTA direction:
- Book an introductory conversation
- Start with a confidential conversation
- Let’s discuss where clarity is needed
